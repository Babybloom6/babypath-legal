Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What Data We Collect

Baby Health Data: Feeding logs, sleep records, diaper changes, growth measurements, health observations, and medical history information you choose to provide.

Caregiver Information: Name, email address, phone number, profile information, and family/caregiver relationships.

Usage Data: App usage patterns, feature interactions, device information, IP address, and analytics data to improve our service.

2. How We Use the Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve BabyPath services
  • Personalize your experience
  • Send service updates and support communications
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve features
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit and at rest. We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, firewalls, and access controls to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

While we strive to protect your data, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing Policy

We NEVER sell your personal data to third parties.

We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating our service, subject to confidentiality agreements. This includes Supabase, which hosts your account and baby data, and third-party AI providers, which process the messages and baby context you send to the AI Parenting Advisor feature in order to generate a response. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

5. Australian Privacy Act 1988 Compliance

BabyPath complies with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Australian privacy legislation.

6. COPPA Compliance

BabyPath complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years old. Parents or guardians who believe we have collected information about a child under 13 should contact us immediately.

Baby health data collected through the app is considered parental information and is not subject to COPPA restrictions.

7. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, BabyPath complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently. You have rights regarding your data as outlined in Section 8.

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Download your data in a portable format
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)

To exercise these rights, contact us at support.babypath@gmail.com.

9. Cookie Policy

BabyPath uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our service.

You can control cookie settings through your device or browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the app.
