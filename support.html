Common questions

How do I invite a caregiver?
Open Team from the bottom navigation and share your invite code with a partner, grandparent, or nanny.

How does the AI Advisor work?
BabyPath AI is powered by Anthropic's Claude models. It answers questions using the feeding, sleep, and diaper data you've logged. It is not a substitute for advice from your pediatrician.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID — go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and data.
